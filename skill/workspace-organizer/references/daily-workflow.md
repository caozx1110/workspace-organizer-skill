# Daily Obsidian and Agent workflow

Read this reference for daily planning, capture, focus selection, or questions
about which page to inspect.

## Human pages

- `01_导航/HOME.md` is the stable user-owned entry point.
- `01_导航/FOCUS.md` is optional and user-owned; keep one to three Task links.
- `00_总览/TODAY.md` combines focus, scheduled and due work, overdue items,
  executable next actions, follow-ups, and a privacy-safe Inbox signal.
- `00_总览/NEXT.md` is the complete open queue, grouped and sorted by actionability.
- `00_总览/INBOX.md` contains untriaged Capture links, not provisional Tasks.
- `00_总览/WAITING.md` separates external waiting and blocked work from actions
  that can be done now.
- `00_总览/ARCHIVE_INDEX.md` links closed bundles by frozen area and year.

`HOME.md` and `FOCUS.md` have no generated marker and must never be replaced.
Other pages are generated projections with a source digest. Daily Notes are
free-form logs, not Task records.

## Routine

Morning: open HOME and TODAY, select at most three focus tasks, check overdue
and follow-up items, and make every active task's next action executable.

During work: capture with natural language, open the Task note for context, and
request a preview before attaching or moving a file. Ordinary note body edits
may happen directly in Obsidian; Agent metadata edits use CAS.

Evening: explicitly complete or cancel outcomes with a closure summary, update
or reschedule unfinished next actions, triage a small Inbox batch, and review
waiting follow-ups. Archive review is separate and can be approved in batches.

Do not dump all open tasks into TODAY. Use NEXT for the full queue. Relative
dates such as “Friday” or “next week” are interpreted in workspace timezone and
must be echoed as concrete dates before writing.
